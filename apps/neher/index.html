A feeder is sized from the device that protects it, not from its connected load: NEC 240.4 requires the conductor to be protected at its ampacity, and the 240.6(A) rating of that device already includes the 125 % continuous-load allowance of 215.2(A)(1) — so the Factor stays at 1.00 to avoid double counting. Exceptions to keep in mind: 240.4(B) allows a device up to 800 A to be the next standard size above the conductor ampacity, and motor circuits (430.52, 240.4(G)) are still sized from the motor FLC — use the MOTOR load type for those.
